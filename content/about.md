I'm a Controls Engineer at [Strider Robotics](https://strider.bot) in Bengaluru, India, where I work on building control systems for legged robots.

## Background

I earned my Master's in Mechanical Engineering from the University of Delaware, where I worked in the [Biorobotics Lab](https://sites.udel.edu/poulakalab/) under the guidance of Prof. Ioannis Poulakakis. My research focused on dynamic locomotion and control of legged robots.

## Interests

My work sits at the intersection of:

- **Robot Dynamics** - Understanding and modeling the physics of robotic systems
- **Control Design** - Developing controllers for dynamic, underactuated systems
- **Optimization** - Using numerical methods to solve control problems in real-time
- **Reinforcement Learning** - Exploring learning-based approaches to robot control

## This Site

I built this site to share technical notes and ideas. Most posts focus on optimization, control theory, and robotics. The site is built with [Hugo](https://gohugo.io) and hosted on GitHub Pages.
