I'm a controls engineer working on robotics at Strider Robotics. This blog is where I write up things I find technically interesting—mostly at the intersection of control systems, optimization, and machine learning.

## What you'll find here

**Control Systems & Robotics** — Model Predictive Control, legged locomotion, real-time trajectory optimization. Practical guides with Python and MuJoCo.

**Optimization** — Interior point methods, quadratic programming, convex optimization as it applies to control and learning problems.

**Reinforcement Learning** — Starting from tabular methods (value iteration, Q-learning, SARSA) and building up toward deep RL. I try to derive everything from first principles.

**Deep Learning** — Occasionally, foundational topics like convolution, optimizers, and architectures when they connect to something I'm working on.

## Who this is for

If you're a researcher, engineer, or student who learns best by seeing the math spelled out alongside working code—this is aimed at you. I try to write posts that I would have wanted to read when I was first learning these topics.

## How the content is organized

Posts are grouped into series where the topics connect. The [Tabular RL](/posts/tabular-rl/) series, for example, covers six algorithms in progression. Browse by [category](/categories/) or [tag](/tags/) to find what's relevant to you.

Subscribe via [RSS](/posts/index.xml) to get new posts as they go up.
